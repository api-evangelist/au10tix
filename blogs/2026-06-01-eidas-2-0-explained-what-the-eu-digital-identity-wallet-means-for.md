eIDAS 2.0 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183) introduces the European Digital Identity Wallet, letting EU citizens store and share verified credentials across borders without repeated identity checks. All 27 Member States must provide certified digital wallets by late 2026, with mandatory private-sector acceptance for regulated industries following in late 2027.
