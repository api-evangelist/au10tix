ETSI TS 119 461 is the European technical standard establishing requirements for identity proofing under the eIDAS regulatory framework, defining verification methods like document authentication, biometric verification, and liveness detection. Mandatory for Qualified Trust Service Providers, it is becoming a competitive requirement for identity verification providers across Europe.
