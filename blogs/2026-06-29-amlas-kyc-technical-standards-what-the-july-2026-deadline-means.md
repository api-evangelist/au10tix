The EU's new Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA) must submit 23 regulatory technical standards to the European Commission by July 10, 2026, harmonizing customer due diligence rules across Member States. The standards cover identity verification methods and acceptable identification sources, becoming directly applicable July 10, 2027.
